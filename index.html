How it works / settings

Pivots: a swing high (low) is a bar whose high (low) is the highest (lowest) of Swing bars on each side. The move since the last confirmed pivot is shown as a tentative pivot (grey label). "TB" = bars of the selected timeframe from the previous pivot; the number after the price is the change from the previous pivot.

Price levels (from the last Depth pivots, several swings each): Ret = retracement of a swing (0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786); EX Ret = external retracement past the swing start (1.272, 1.618, 2.618); App = alternate price projection, swing A→B length projected from C (0.618, 1.000, 1.618). Only levels not yet traded through since their anchor pivot are drawn (tick Hit lvls to show all). A wick through a level counts. Levels just beyond the most recent pivot’s extreme that price came near but never traded through (within one average bar range; a low exactly at the level counts as a test) are marked held: bolder, labelled “held”, with a dot on the pivot bar. Lines start at the anchor pivot bar. Two or more levels inside Zone % of price form a bold zone label (e.g. 7703-16).

DTP time pane: counts time projections landing on each bar, counted in bars of the selected timeframe (future slots skip weekends and main NYSE holidays; intraday US stocks/indexes use the 9:30–16:00 ET session, futures ≈ Sun 18:00–Fri 17:00 ET with the daily 17:00–18:00 break, crypto 24×7; half-days are not modeled): time retracements/extensions of prior swings (0.382–2.618), alternate time projections (A→B duration from C: 0.618, 1, 1.618) and same-kind cycles (low→low, high→high: 0.618–1.618). Bar height = number of projections coinciding. Auto targets the pivot the current swing is heading for.

Mouse: wheel = zoom, drag = pan, Shift+wheel or drag on the price axis = vertical scale, double-click = reset. Touch: drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

Ticker shortcuts: SPX → ^GSPC, NDX, DJI, RUT, VIX, ES/NQ/YM/RTY/CL/GC/SI → futures, BTC/ETH. Data: Financial Modeling Prep (Yahoo Finance fallback) — weekly, daily, 60m, 30m, 15m.
