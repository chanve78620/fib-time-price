How it works / settings

Pivots: a swing high (low) is a bar whose high (low) is the highest (lowest) of Swing bars on each side. The move since the last confirmed pivot is shown as a tentative pivot (grey label). "TB" = trading bars from the previous pivot; the number after the price is the change from the previous pivot.

Price levels (from the last Depth pivots, several swings each): Ret = retracement of a swing (0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786); EX Ret = external retracement past the swing start (1.272, 1.618, 2.618); App = alternate price projection, swing A→B length projected from C (0.618, 1.000, 1.618). Only levels not yet traded through since their anchor pivot are drawn (tick Hit lvls to show all). Lines start at the anchor pivot bar. Two or more levels inside Zone % of price form a bold zone label (e.g. 7703-16).

DTP time pane: counts time projections landing on each bar, counted in trading days (weekends and main NYSE holidays skipped for future dates): time retracements/extensions of prior swings (0.382–2.618), alternate time projections (A→B duration from C: 0.618, 1, 1.618) and same-kind cycles (low→low, high→high: 0.618–1.618). Bar height = number of projections coinciding. Auto targets the pivot the current swing is heading for.

Mouse: wheel = zoom, drag = pan, Shift+wheel or drag on the price axis = vertical scale, double-click = reset. Touch: drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

Ticker shortcuts: SPX → ^GSPC, NDX, DJI, RUT, VIX, ES/NQ/YM/RTY/CL/GC/SI → futures, BTC/ETH. Data: Yahoo Finance daily bars.
